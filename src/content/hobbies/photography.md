## 摄影 / Photography

周末喜欢带着相机出门，记录城市的角落和自然的美好。偶尔也会研究 Lightroom 调色。

On weekends I like heading out with a camera to capture the corners of the city and the beauty of nature, occasionally tinkering with Lightroom colour grading.

### 常用器材 / Gear

- Sony A7M4 + 24-70mm f/2.8
- DJI Mini 4 Pro 无人机 / drone
- iPhone 随时记录 / always on hand
